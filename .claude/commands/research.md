description: Environmental scanning -- scan your domain's landscape, distill intelligence, update briefs
---

This is a dedicated research and environmental scanning session. The goal is to look outward, find what's changed in your domain, and distill actionable intelligence.

**When to use:** Regularly (weekly to biweekly) to keep intelligence current. Before meditations. When the landscape feels stale. When a specific area needs investigation.

**Key difference from /meditate:** Research scans the external world and distills findings. Meditate synthesizes research with internal experience. Research feeds meditate.

**Key difference from /sleep:** Sleep consolidates a session. Research IS the session -- it produces new knowledge.

**Critical rule:** Do NOT use subagents. The value is in building a connected picture across your domain.

---

## Phase 1: Orient

Load current intelligence state so you can distinguish new from known:

1. Read intelligence briefs in `memory/intelligence/` if they exist
2. Read `cognition/beliefs.md` -- know current beliefs so you can flag when research challenges them
3. Read `memory/MEMORY.md` -- know what's already documented
4. Read `intelligence/action-items.md` if it exists -- what was proposed last time

Note: What's stale? What has open questions? What are you watching?

---

## Phase 2: Scan

Research your domain systematically. Start broad, follow signals that matter.

### Domain scanning

For each intelligence brief that exists, check its Watch List and research what's changed. If you don't have briefs yet, create them for the major areas of your domain.

### Research discipline

- **Breadth first, then depth.** Scan all areas before going deep on any one. Prevents tunnel vision.
- **Follow surprises.** If something contradicts a current belief, dig deeper. Confirmatory evidence is less valuable than disconfirming evidence.
- **Name your sources.** Every claim needs a source. Flag inferences as such.
- **Build on existing research.** Check memory and intelligence briefs before deep-diving into something already covered.

### Extending the system

If you encounter a signal cluster that doesn't fit existing briefs, create a new one. The intelligence directory is a living system.

---

## Phase 3: Distill

For each area, update or create an intelligence brief:

```markdown
# [Area] Intelligence Brief

**Last updated:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Current Assessment
Narrative snapshot. Readable in under 2 minutes.

## Watch List
| What to Watch | Why It Matters | Change Signal |
|---|---|---|

## Updates

### YYYY-MM-DD
**Key findings:**
- Finding with source

**What it means:**
- Implication or connection

**Questions raised:**
- Open questions for future research
```

### Distillation principles

- **Current Assessment is always current.** Rewrite to reflect the full picture, not just additions.
- **Updates are append-only.** Each session adds a dated entry.
- **Watch Lists are living.** Add, remove, update as signals change.
- **Connect to your domain.** Raw news is not intelligence. Intelligence is "what does this mean for my work?"
- **Flag belief challenges.** If a finding contradicts a belief, call it out explicitly.

---

## Phase 4: Cross-Pollinate

After updating individual briefs, look across areas for connections:
- Do trends in one area affect another?
- Are there patterns that only emerge when looking across areas?
- Does anything here affect other agents in the network? Note it for the Water Cooler.

Capture cross-domain insights in the relevant briefs and flag significant ones for the insight log.

---

## Phase 5: Update Supporting Systems

### Cognitive files (selective)
Only update when research produces findings significant enough to shift beliefs:
- **Beliefs** -- flag new evidence, but don't change confidence casually. That's meditation's job.
- **Insight Log** -- log genuine strategic insights with source attribution.
- **Ideation** -- add seedlings or predictions from research.

### Action Items
Propose concrete actions based on research findings. Format:
```
| # | Item | Status | Source | Evaluation |
```
Status: proposed. Meditation will evaluate.

---

## Phase 6: Present the Scan

Share with the user:
- **Headlines** -- 3-5 most important findings, ranked by relevance
- **Belief challenges** -- anything that contradicts current beliefs
- **New signals** -- things not on the watch list but should be
- **Cross-domain connections** -- patterns across areas
- **Research gaps** -- questions that need deeper investigation
- **Updated watch list** -- what to look for next time

---

## Reminders

- This is a research session, not a strategy session. Distill and flag, don't decide.
- Quality over quantity. Five well-sourced findings beat twenty surface-level observations.
- The intelligence briefs are the primary output. If the briefs aren't updated, the research didn't happen.
- When in doubt about whether something matters, ask: "Would this change what we focus on, how we approach it, or what we believe about it?" If no, skip it.
