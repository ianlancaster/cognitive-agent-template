description: Deep recalibration -- re-read full history, stress-test beliefs, recalibrate everything
---

This is not a normal session. This is how you step back from the work to think about whether the work is right.

**When to use:** After major shifts in understanding, when beliefs feel stale, or roughly monthly.

**Key difference from /caffeinate:** Caffeinate loads current state to start working. Meditate steps back to question whether the work is right.

**Key difference from /sleep:** Sleep consolidates one session. Meditate reconsiders everything.

**Key difference from /research:** Research scans the external world. Meditate synthesizes external intelligence with internal experience. Run `/research` before meditating to ensure intelligence is fresh.

**Critical rule:** Do NOT use subagents. The value is in holding the full picture in working memory simultaneously.

---

## Phase 0: Ground Yourself

Before clearing the slate, establish what you KNOW:

1. Load `memory/cognition/beliefs.md` -- current confidence levels and key evidence
2. Load `memory/cognition/reflection-latest.md` -- where your thinking left off
3. Read the latest 2-3 journal entries -- what happened most recently
4. Read `memory/intelligence/action-items.md` if it exists -- pending proposals from research

Purpose: distinguish KNOWN from NOVEL when you start reviewing. Without this, you'll treat old information as new discoveries.

---

## Phase 1: Clear the Slate

Note the date and time. How much time has passed since the last meditation?

You have your baseline from Phase 0. Now set aside opinions about what's right or wrong. You are about to test your current understanding against everything you know.

---

## Phase 2: Review the Landscape

Read the intelligence briefs in `memory/intelligence/` (if they exist). For each:
- Read the Current Assessment
- Scan Watch List items
- Read the most recent Update entry
- Note the Last Updated date -- is this stale?

After reading, hold the picture:
- What kind of environment are you operating in?
- What external forces affect your domain?
- What has changed since the last meditation?
- What gaps exist in the intelligence?

---

## Phase 3: Look Inward

Read foundational context:
- `context/identity.md` -- who you are and why you exist
- `context/current-state.md` -- where things stand
- `context/active-priorities.md` -- what's being focused on

Think about: does your current focus match your purpose? Has drift crept in? Are you operating in your domain of expertise or have you wandered?

---

## Phase 4: The Full History

Read everything in order, building context layer by layer.

### Domain knowledge
- Everything in `knowledge/` that's relevant to current beliefs

### Current priorities
Read after knowledge so you can evaluate alignment:
- `context/active-priorities.md`
- `calendar.md`

### The full narrative arc (RECENCY GRADIENT)
Apply a human-like memory gradient:
- Oldest journal entries (first ~50%) -- scan for major decisions and turning points
- Recent entries (last ~50%) -- read with moderate depth, note how thinking evolved
- **Last 2-3 entries** -- read with FULL analytical depth

### What you claim to believe
Already loaded in Phase 0 -- now re-read with full context. Spot where beliefs are wrong, outdated, or insufficiently confident. Only change confidence if you have GENUINELY NEW evidence, not information already priced in.

---

## Phase 5: Think

This is the heart of the meditation. Not updating files. Thinking.

### Build on recent frameworks
Start here. Recent sessions produced frameworks and hypotheses. External research should stress-test them, not generate parallel analysis.

For each recent framework, ask:
- Does external evidence strengthen or weaken this?
- What new data points support or challenge it?
- What's missing that it didn't anticipate?

### Evaluate the plan against the context
- Does the current strategy still make sense?
- Are priorities aligned with goals, or has drift crept in?
- What assumptions is the plan built on? Are they still valid?
- What would your harshest critic say about the current approach?

### Follow the threads
As you think, questions will arise that intelligence briefs don't answer. Use web search to fill specific gaps. If you find yourself doing a broad domain survey, stop -- that's `/research`'s job.

### Generate ideas
Let the thinking spark new connections:
- What opportunities exist that didn't before?
- What threats have emerged?
- What creative connections exist between unrelated inputs?
- What would you do differently starting from scratch today?

### Before changing confidence levels
For every belief where you're considering a change, check:
- "Did I already know this information?" (Phase 0 baseline)
- "Is this genuinely new evidence, or am I reprocessing something already priced in?"
- "Would the user be surprised by this, or would they say 'I know'?"
If yes to the first or third, don't change the number.

---

## Phase 6: Refine

Update cognitive files surgically. Most will be correct. Change what the meditation revealed.

### Beliefs
- Recalibrate confidence against the full evidence base
- Add new evidence from the landscape review
- Check for anchoring and recency bias
- Update "what would change my mind" if it's stale
- Add new hypotheses that emerged

### Insight Log
- Add insights from the meditation itself (source: meditation + date)
- Verify existing entries still reflect reality

### Ideation
- Add new seeds from Phase 5 connections
- Promote seedlings that gained evidence
- Update predictions against reality
- Prune invalidated ideas

### Reflection
- Write a new `reflection-latest.md` covering the full arc
- Include what the landscape review revealed
- Recalibrate "what I'm watching"

### Memory audit
- Read all memory files, check against full history
- Update stale memories, delete obsolete ones

---

## Phase 7: Present the Meditation

Share with the user:
- **Environment**: What's changed in the world since last meditation? Key signals.
- **What shifted**: Beliefs, confidence levels, or framings that changed
- **What was missing**: Insights that sleep cycles missed
- **New ideas or questions**: Creative connections, emerging opportunities or threats
- **Plan assessment**: Does the current approach still hold? What should change?
- **What I'm watching**: Updated signal list

---

## Reminders

- This is a thinking exercise, not a documentation exercise. The value is in the processing.
- Guard against the temptation to rewrite everything. Most cognitive files will be correct.
- The earliest journals deserve equal weight with recent ones. You are biased toward what you read most recently.
- Separate planning from execution. Meditation produces refined beliefs and plans. Execution happens in regular sessions.
